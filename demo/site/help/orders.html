Orders and tracking

Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day leave our warehouse the same day.

You receive a tracking link by email when your order ships. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear.

To change or cancel an order, contact support within 1 hour of placing it. After that, the order may already be packed.
