# Northwind Kettles care guide

Fictional demo business; synthetic content.

## Descaling

Descale your kettle every 4 weeks in hard-water areas, and every 8 weeks elsewhere.
Fill it halfway with equal parts water and white vinegar, or use one sachet of citric acid.
Boil, leave it for 30 minutes, then rinse three times with fresh water.

## Cleaning

Wipe the outside with a damp cloth. Never put a kettle or its base in the dishwasher,
and never immerse the base in water.

## Filters

The Aurora Glass Kettle has a removable limescale filter. Rinse it weekly and replace it every 6 months.
